## Main weaknesses (for the arXiv bar)
1. **Notebook form, not paper form.** It has `[ours]` tags, session language, a "Roberto's motivation" paragraph, no author line, no code or data statement and no AI-use statement.
   It has only two figures; most results are inline tables. A reader can't reproduce anything without the repo.
2. **Two papers in one.** Part I is a modest, checkable methods note. Part II is an open research programme whose central computation hasn't converged.
   Together they read as a progress report.
3. **Everything quantitative is 1+1.** Fine for a methods note if said up front. Fatal for any claim about our universe (C6, 3+1 memory numbers are scalings, not computations).
4. **Small-N quantum results** (N ≤ 8 for records, N ≤ 11 for F_N) with birth-order alternatives. The weak-β drift could be a finite-size effect.
5. **Sampler problems:** the N = 400 "hysteresis" is partly a frozen chain, and the A1 sampling is sign-problem-limited. Both are labelled, but a referee would want parallel tempering.
6. **No prediction**, and the C3 cutoff is invisible to wave propagation in 1+1.
