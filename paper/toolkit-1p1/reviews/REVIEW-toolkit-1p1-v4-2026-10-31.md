# Honest review — toolkit-1p1-v4 (2026-10-31, session 19)

**What changed:** referee round 3, comments 18–24 (see `answers/answer-toolkit-1p1-v4.md`). The abstract now matches Sec. IV, and r41 reruns the one-region comparison.

**Assessment.**
- The paper now presents the curvature estimator at its true strength. It is unbiased as an ensemble average under a position-based selection. On a single region it is about 3× noisier than the ordering fraction in de Sitter space, slightly noisier in flat space, and biased low.
- The contribution is the exact identity, the distance estimator and the closed-form drift (analytic and conceptual), plus the selection-bias point. This is thinner than in v2. The referee still judges it sufficient for CQG.
- The one test that could restore a practical case, locality (referee 24), was not run.
- I found one thing the referee did not ask for: the v3 one-region pairs were drawn with the biased mechanism the paper itself names. The rerun showed it did not matter here, but it should have been caught in session 18 (lesson 50).
