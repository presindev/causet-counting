Strengths: one clear question; paired comparison on 44 universes, 44/44 wins; oracles that locate the error; ablations, convergence and path tests; limits stated.
Risks: (1) significance — better accuracy on a flat benchmark, no downstream use shown (said openly); a PRD referee may call it incremental or "better for CQG".
(2) The likelihood is Casasola's (Research Square, not peer reviewed); what is ours is start + test + ablations. (3) Johnston's Table IV not reproduced, unexplained.
(4) Slower than Johnston up to n ≈ 10⁴ in the main form. (5) Readability: the valley paragraph of Sec. V is ~400 words of numbers; a reader will skim it.
Small: "the gap does not close" (gap to what); "6–8 %" (bias or median error); "the map no longer changes" vs ΔC ~1–2×10⁻⁴.
