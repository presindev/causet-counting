- Abstract, storyline, discussion items (viii)–(x), limitations, next steps and six new dropped paths. 14 new references.

## What is (maybe) ours
- As before: rectangle identity, depth-drift curvature, calibrated detector.
- Laguerre form at fixed n: one-line consequence of known pieces; "not found" in 13 full texts, INSPIRE not searched.
- Future-factor criterion: natural, but quantum-growth people (Surya, Zalel, Dowker) may have it in another form. INSPIRE crawl still owed (session 8 note).
- Endpoint-phase result for B: elementary linear algebra; surely known in spirit (phases from a path-independent functional are a gauge). Useful for us, not news.
- Rung-2 test and screen test: our constructions; the conclusions are small-N or 1+1 only.

## Main weaknesses
1. **Two storylines are now in one paper**: "is it spacetime?" (Secs. 2–5) and "what must a growing universe remember?" (Sec. 6). They connect through the growth
   step, but the abstract has to do a lot. A future split into two notes may read better.
2. **Section 6's quantum part rests on N ≤ 8** with birth-order alternatives, which are not covariant. The direction of the effect (phases add memory / make records)
   could change with stems or at larger N. It is labelled, but it is the weakest part.
3. The screen ("what the last-born event sees") and the records were chosen by us, not derived. The coherence measure weights all pairs equally.
4. The memory-ladder area rung is a statement about GR/CKN, not something our model does. It is a map, not a result.
5. Still 1+1 for everything quantitative; no 3+1 content; the F_N result at N = 11 is pending.
6. The motivation for the records paragraph ("QM as memory saving") is Roberto's idea, presented as motivation. Correct, but a reader may find it speculative next to
   numerical sections. It is clearly marked `[ours]`.

## What would upgrade it
- F_N at N = 11 (running on the cluster).
- The screen test with covariant stems and a fixed per-event view budget, and record counts per patch vs births.
- An INSPIRE citation crawl for the Laguerre target and F_N before any claim leaves the notebook.

## Overall
A coherent notebook paper: a positive toolkit, a first particle probe that cleanly separates phases, and a memory analysis that is mostly negative but sharp
(volume law; no classical macrostate; phases don't save memory at small N). No prediction, no 3+1. Not ready for outside readers; a good map of where we stand.
