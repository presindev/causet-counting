# Honest review of draft v3 (draft-v3.pdf, 2026-10-25)

## Big picture
New storyline: "is it spacetime?" Rules → cosmological cutoff (condensed from v2) → toolkit for reading geometry
from the order alone (time, space, curvature, shape) → apply it to grown, e^{-βS}-weighted and quantum-weighted
universes. Much more concrete than v2: every section now has a calculation that was validated on a known answer.

## What is (maybe) ours
- Rectangle identity d² = 2V_A V_B / V_C in 1+1, valid for any reference event. Exact, elementary, 1+1 only.
  Not found in a brief search; could easily be known to the people working on BK overlaps.
- Curvature from the depth drift of the distance estimate, 1 − Rτ²/16. Validated on de Sitter. Noisy per pair.
- An intrinsic, calibrated manifold detector (MDS spectrum + stress), with its size threshold (N ≳ 100–200).
  The method is MDS (known); the calibration and the use as a detector are ours.
- Sampling quantum record probabilities at N = 100, and the bound |A_r| ≤ #r (likely known).

## Main weaknesses
1. Everything is 1+1; C6 (Newton/Einstein) needs 3+1 and the rectangle identity does not survive there.
2. The quantum result is fluctuation-level: the record (stress quartile) separates fluctuations among genuine
   sprinklings, not sheets from crystals. The "no drift" is a null result with ±0.03–0.06 errors.
3. Choices not derived: ε = 0.21, the record, the detector thresholds.
4. Curvature estimator needs ~100 pairs for ±0.4 — fine as a tool, not as precision.
5. N = 400 crystallisation unresolved; β_c scaling extrapolation failed.
6. Matter still absent; the cutoff still has no observable consequence.

## What would upgrade it
- Quantum sampling at N = 200 with a record that actually distinguishes phases (e.g. layer count), on the cluster.
- The detector in 2+1 with the general BK estimator.
- A literature check by someone who knows the BK follow-ups (2025 "Local d'Alembertian", 2026 "Reconstructing
  Minkowski geometry from causal separations") for the rectangle identity.
