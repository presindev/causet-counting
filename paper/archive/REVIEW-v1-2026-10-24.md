# Honest review of draft v1 (draft-v1.pdf, 2026-10-24)

## Big picture
One storyline now: rules R1–R3 → neighbours = 1 per unit rapidity (known) → finite universe caps rapidity at
η_max ≈ 142 (our addition) → background-free toy model confirms counts and gives intrinsic proper-time fuzziness
→ growth makes η_max(t). Readable, honest, mostly correct. Still no new physics result.

## What is (maybe) ours
- Finite universe as the physical regulator of causal-set non-locality + accepting the cosmological frame as
  the frame it produces. Not found in brief search.
- Elementary derivations (1 link per unit rapidity; links = records = harmonic numbers) — presentation only.

## Main weaknesses
1. In 3+1 the cutoff leaves ~10^124 neighbours: the "taming" largely fails in the physical dimension.
2. No observable consequence (η_max far beyond tests; leakage would need dynamics).
3. Relies on sprinkling (background-assisted generation), no dynamics.
4. "Size of the universe" not yet defined relationally.

## What would upgrade it
A relational definition of L in a growing causal set, plus any computable low-energy effect of finite valency
(e.g. on Sorkin's nonlocal d'Alembertian).
