# Review against the PRD bar — counting-full-v2 (2026-10-29, session 14)

Separate from the arXiv-bar reviews. Decision (Roberto): wait for Boguñá's reply before choosing the journal route.

**Short answer:** the 3+1 half could be a PRD paper; the current combined draft probably is not (expect "major revision" or rejection for lack of focus, not for errors).

## What PRD would see as the contribution
PRD is a normal venue for causal-set methods papers (Boguñá–Krioukov 2024, Johnston 2025, Shuman). Our core clears the bar in kind: a diagnosis of where a 2025 PRD
method fails (low-count spacelike pairs), a counting argument for why inverting small counts cannot fix it, and a fix that removes 79–83 % of its residual in 21/21
universes with no decay to n = 15000; plus honest credit (Casasola), an ablation against our own term, held-out tests, a second shape, public code.

## What a referee would push on (most serious first)
1. **Two papers in one.** The 1+1 toolbox (rectangle identity, depth drift, detector calibration, Laguerre target) and the 3+1 embedding share a theme, not a result.
2. **"You applied someone else's likelihood."** The fit is Casasola's; ML coordinates are standard. Ours: diagnosis, counting argument, intrinsic start, benchmark.
   This must be argued up front. Casasola is an unrefereed Research Square preprint.
3. **No physical payoff yet.** Flat Minkowski diamonds, sprinkled (manifold-like by construction); time coordinate not refitted; no curvature or dynamical causal sets in 3+1.
4. **The gain looks small in the metric used** (ρ 0.9886 → 0.9980 at n = 15000). Per-pair distance error in discreteness lengths would make it concrete; not reported yet.
5. **Prominent AI-method section** is unusual for PRD (APS disclosure is met); some referees will find it distracting, and PRD is not where that result gets credit.
6. **Language** (Roberto's pass planned).

## What would raise the chances (cheapest first)
1. Refocus (writing only): the 3+1 storyline as the paper, the 1+1 tools as a short section or companion note — or keep the toolbox and target **CQG** instead.
2. Per-pair error in discreteness lengths (laptop rerun at n = 3000, < 1 h).
3. One physical test in 3+1: a curved (de Sitter) sprinkling, to show the flat-volume bias (a few sessions).
4. Method story to an appendix for the journal version; full on arXiv and in the repo; a separate short piece where the AI-for-science result counts.

## Recommendation
arXiv as is after the language pass. Journal: (a) PRD after refocusing on 3+1 + items 2–4 (preferred: the 3+1 result is the one people will use), or (b) CQG now with
the toolbox. Decide after Boguñá's reply: if he finds the 3+1 part useful, that is the best evidence it clears the bar.
